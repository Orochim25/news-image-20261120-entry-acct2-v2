A newspaper front page picturing **35 verified stories from Friday 20 November 2026, across 7 regions** — Europe 11, Asia 8, Global 4, Africa 4, Americas 4, Oceania 2, Middle East 2.

Three photographic heroes lead the page: bridges hit in Kyiv (at least eight killed), Cyclone Akvan's landfall near Gopalpur (300,000+ evacuated), and Asia's AI sell-off (Taiwan −6.2%, worst week since 1991). All eight "Day in Brief" scenes are AI photographs too, followed by 24 more stories and a date-check box that keeps 19 November events — and not-yet events — out of the picture.

A small world map in the bottom-left corner, "Where the news happened", plots 16 story locations as dots at their fixed latitude/longitude coordinates — Kyiv, Gopalpur, Taipei, Seoul, Tokyo, London, Brussels, Warsaw, Rome, Nairobi, Wellington, New York, Lviv, Berlin, Abuja and Baghdad.

Every figure is attributed to its source (Reuters, AP, Al Jazeera, IMD, ICC, ATP, Investopedia, CNN). The program (`main.py`, standard library only) copies `entry.png` into the output directory.
