A newspaper front page for one day: **Friday 20 November 2026, 35 verified stories from 7 regions** — Europe 11, Asia 8, Global 4, Africa 4, Americas 4, Oceania 2, Middle East 2.

Three photographic heroes lead the page: Russian missiles hit three bridges in Kyiv (at least eight killed across Ukraine, Reuters), Cyclone Akvan makes landfall near Gopalpur with 300,000+ evacuated (IMD), and Asia's AI sell-off — Taiwan −6.2%, its worst week since 1991 (Al Jazeera). An illustrated grid and a 24-item "Also Today" list cover the rest of the day, every figure carrying its source and date.

A date-check box keeps the record honest: four big stories from Thursday 19 November (the ICJ ruling on Myanmar, the Trump–Xi–Putin declaration in Shenzhen, Tonga's drought emergency, the Senegal boat) are excluded, and two things that had not happened yet — BepiColombo's Mercury orbit insertion and the final COP31 text — are not claimed.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python standard library only) copies `entry.png` into `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`), refusing to copy if the file exceeds the 1,048,576-byte limit.
