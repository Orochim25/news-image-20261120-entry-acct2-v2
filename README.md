# The World in One Day — Friday 20 November 2026

Thirty-three verified stories from a single Friday, drawn as one
newspaper page on a faint globe. Three lead stories carry realistic
AI news photographs: the burning bridges of Kyiv, Cyclone Akvan over
the Bay of Bengal, and the red candlesticks of Asia's AI sell-off.
Ten further stories fill the grid below — a UK–EU reset deal, the
last scheduled day of COP31, a Russian missile in Poland, Iraq's army
in Jurf al-Sakhar, a general strike in Italy, Japanese inflation at
2.3%, an impeachment move in Seoul, Kenya's first oil well, Glenn
Phillips' 118 in Wellington, and Britain's cyber chief — and twenty
short briefs run down the right-hand side, beside a world map whose
thirty-three numbered pins mark where each story happened.

Every item on the page is dated Friday 20 November 2026: it either
happened that day or was first reported then, and any figure that
comes from officials is labelled as such. Thursday's big stories
were deliberately kept off the page — the World Court's Myanmar
ruling, the three-leader declaration from Shenzhen, Tonga's drought
emergency, and the migrant boat lost off Senegal — and the red DATE
CHECK box at the foot of the page says so, along with the two things
that had not happened yet: BepiColombo's arrival in orbit around
Mercury, and a final COP31 text.

`entry.png` is the poster itself (1024×1024 PNG). `main.py` is the
entrypoint: plain Python 3 with no third-party imports, it copies
`entry.png` into `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`) and
verifies the copy is byte-identical to the file shipped here.
